How users consent

SafeRides collects SMS consent during account signup, inside the SafeRides rider app and the SafeRides Driver app. The interactive demonstration below reproduces the consent screen exactly as it appears to users, at Step 3 of 6 of signup. The screen is identical in both apps.

The checkbox is unchecked by default. The user must actively check it to consent. Account verification is performed by email, not by text message. SafeRides does not purchase, rent, or acquire phone numbers from data brokers or any third-party list vendor.
